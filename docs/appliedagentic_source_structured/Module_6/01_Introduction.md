# What Is "The Last Mile"?

In logistics, the "last mile" refers to the final leg of delivery — getting a package from a distribution centre to someone's front door. It's often the most *expensive*, most *complex*, and most *failure-prone* part of the entire journey.

The same phenomenon occurs in AI deployment. Building an AI system that works in a lab environment is often straightforward. **Getting it to work reliably in the real world, adopted by real people, integrated into real systems** — that's the last mile. And it's where most AI projects quietly fail.

> 💡 **What This Means**: A pilot that succeeds in a controlled test environment can still fail spectacularly when deployed at scale. Module 6 is about understanding why — and what to do about it.

## What This Module Covers

### 📞 Voice Agents: The Healthcare Case Study
We begin with a cautionary tale: a healthcare clinic that deployed a voice AI agent, watched it succeed in testing, and saw it fail dramatically in production. By examining *exactly what went wrong*, we build the intuition needed to avoid similar mistakes.

We balance this with a real-world success story — showing what *good* voice agent deployment looks like.

### 🚧 Last-Mile Integration: Why Pilots Succeed but Deployments Stall
Why do so many AI projects that pass pilot testing never make it to full deployment? We analyse the systemic reasons — technical, organisational, and political — and offer strategies for crossing the implementation chasm.

### 👥 Internal Resistance and Change Management
Technology is only half the equation. The human side — fear, scepticism, power dynamics, job security concerns — is equally important. We explore proven frameworks for managing organisational change during AI adoption.

### 📊 Monitoring Agent Performance: KPIs and Feedback Loops
Deploying an AI agent isn't a "set it and forget it" moment. How do you know it's working? How do you catch when it starts drifting from its intended purpose? We cover the metrics, KPIs, and feedback loop design that keeps AI deployments on track.

# Why This Module Matters

> ⚠️ **The Hard Truth**: Research consistently shows that the majority of enterprise AI projects either fail to launch or fail to scale. The problem is rarely the technology itself — it's the human, organisational, and integration challenges that surround it.

This module gives you the tools to be in the minority that succeeds.

# Key Takeaways

- The "last mile" of AI deployment — real-world integration with real users — is harder than building the technology
- Pilot success does not guarantee deployment success
- Human and organisational factors (resistance, culture, trust) are as critical as technical ones
- Ongoing monitoring and feedback loops are essential — AI systems need maintenance and oversight, not just launch
