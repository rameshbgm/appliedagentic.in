# Module 6: Learning Objectives

**Summary:** By the end of Module 6 — *The Last Mile: From Pilot to Practice* — you will have developed four practical capabilities that matter enormously in real-world AI deployment.

# What You Will Be Able to Do

By the end of Module 6 — *The Last Mile: From Pilot to Practice* — you will have developed four practical capabilities that matter enormously in real-world AI deployment.


### 🎯 Objective 1: Learn from Failure

**You will be able to:** Study failed AI implementations and use them to identify the pitfalls that leaders most commonly encounter.

**In plain terms:** After this module, when you hear about an AI deployment that went wrong, you won't just see a failure — you'll see *why* it failed and what specific decisions could have changed the outcome. Failure is one of the most efficient teachers available.


### 🎯 Objective 2: Diagnose Last-Mile Integration Challenges

**You will be able to:** Analyse the challenges that cause AI pilot projects to stall before reaching full-scale deployment.

**In plain terms:** You'll understand the specific technical and organisational traps — poor system integration, missing escalation paths, inadequate testing — that turn promising pilots into abandoned projects.


### 🎯 Objective 3: Manage Internal Resistance

**You will be able to:** Identify common sources of human resistance to AI agent deployment and propose evidence-based change management strategies.

**In plain terms:** You'll know how to have the hard conversations — with worried staff, sceptical managers, and resistant departments — and how to design change processes that bring people along rather than leaving them behind.


### 🎯 Objective 4: Design Performance Monitoring Frameworks

**You will be able to:** Design a framework for monitoring AI agent performance using appropriate metrics, KPIs, and feedback loop structures.

**In plain terms:** You'll be able to answer the question "Is our AI actually working?" with data — and design the systems that catch when things go wrong before they become crises.

# How These Skills Connect

| Objective | What You Learn | Why It Matters |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| Learn from failure | Case study analysis | Avoid repeating common mistakes |
| Diagnose stalls | Last-mile integration patterns | Bridge the pilot-to-production gap |
| Manage resistance | Change management frameworks | Achieve human adoption, not just technical deployment |
| Monitor performance | KPI and feedback loop design | Keep AI systems on track over time |

# Key Takeaways

- This module is highly *practical* — focused on real-world deployment challenges, not just theory
- All four objectives connect to each other: you can't monitor what you don't deploy, and you can't deploy without managing resistance
- The skills here apply not just to AI — they're valuable for any complex organisational change
