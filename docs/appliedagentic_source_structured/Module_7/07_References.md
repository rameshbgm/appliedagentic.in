# Recommended Further Reading

- **EU AI Act (Full Text)** — European Parliament (2023) — the landmark regulation categorising AI systems by risk level
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** — nist.gov — the US government's structured approach to identifying and managing AI risk
- **ISO/IEC 42001 Standard** — iso.org — the international standard for AI management systems
- **"How to Build Accountability Into Your AI"** — Sanford (2021), Harvard Business Review — practical leadership guidance on AI accountability
- **McKinsey's AI Compliance Guide for Banks** — Agarwal et al. (2024) — excellent model for regulated-industry AI governance
- **Prosci ADKAR Model** — prosci.com — foundational change management model referenced in this module
