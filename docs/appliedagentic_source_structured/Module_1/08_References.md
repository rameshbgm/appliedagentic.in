## How to Use These References

These sources are organized to support different learning approaches:

- **For business context**: Start with the McKinsey, BCG, and Harvard Business Review pieces — they situate AI capabilities within organizational strategy
- **For technical depth**: The Stöffelbauer and Mozilla guides offer accessible explanations of how LLMs and transformers work
- **For current events and case studies**: The Forbes, Reuters, and Business Insider articles bring real-world examples to the concepts covered in this module
- **For compliance and risk**: The Germanos et al. paper on voice assistant privacy and the OpenAI system card provide important grounding on limitations and risks

# Key Takeaways

1. **Module 1 draws on a rich body of research** — from academic papers to industry reports to journalism — reflecting the multi-disciplinary nature of the AI field.
2. **Foundational sources** like the Goodfellow et al. (2014) GAN paper and Google's "Attention Is All You Need" transformer paper represent the landmark technical breakthroughs that made modern AI possible.
3. **Business-focused sources** from McKinsey, BCG, Forbes, and Gartner translate technical capability into organizational strategy and real-world impact.
4. **Staying current matters** — the AI field moves extremely fast. The sources listed here (validated for 2025) represent the state of knowledge at the time of writing; continuous reading of trusted sources is essential for staying informed.
