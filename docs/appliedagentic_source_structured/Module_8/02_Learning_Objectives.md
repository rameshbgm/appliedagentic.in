# What You Will Learn in Module 8

Module 8 — *Ethics and Capstone* — has two specific learning objectives:


### Objective 1: Evaluate the Ethical Component of Incorporating AI Into Your Organisation

**What this means in plain English**: You will develop the ability to think critically about the human and societal consequences of AI deployment — not just the technical and financial ones.

**Why this matters**: AI is not morally neutral. The same technology that improves efficiency can also:
- Displace workers without adequate transition support
- Encode historical biases into automated decisions
- Create accountability gaps when things go wrong
- Undermine trust if deployed without transparency

Leaders who can evaluate these risks are far more likely to deploy AI responsibly — and to build AI systems that employees, customers, and regulators will actually trust.

**What you'll be able to do**: Apply four core ethical principles — fairness, transparency, accountability, and privacy — to real AI use cases in your organisation.


### Objective 2: Consolidate Course Content, Fortify Connections Between Modules, and Establish a Structure for Writing the Capstone

**What this means in plain English**: You will see how all eight modules connect into a coherent whole, and use that understanding to write a structured AI implementation plan.

**Why this matters**: AI transformation is not a series of separate decisions — it is an integrated journey. The governance choices you make in Module 7 affect the security posture from Module 4. The change management approach from Module 6 must account for the workforce concerns raised by Module 8's ethics content.

The capstone is where these connections become real and actionable.

**What you'll be able to do**: Produce a 2–5 page document that maps AI integration into a real business function, addressing technology, cost, security, change management, governance, and KPIs.

# How Ethics and the Capstone Connect

The two objectives are deliberately linked:

| Ethics teaches you... | The Capstone asks you to... |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------|
| Who is affected by AI decisions | Name the stakeholders your proposal impacts |
| How bias enters AI systems | Describe how you'll audit for bias |
| What transparency requires | Explain how decisions will be communicated |
| Why accountability must be assigned | Identify who owns AI outcomes in your plan |
| How job displacement should be managed | Include a change management and training strategy |

> 💡 **Key Insight**: The capstone is not an add-on. It is the culmination of the entire course — and ethics is the final quality check that turns a technically sound proposal into a *responsible* one.
