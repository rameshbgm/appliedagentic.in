# What This Module Is About

You've reached the final module of the course. Module 8 has two intertwined purposes:

1. **Ethics** — Examining the deeper moral questions that AI raises for individuals, businesses, and society
2. **The Capstone** — Bringing together everything you've learned into a single, structured AI implementation plan

These two elements belong together intentionally. Before you propose how AI should be deployed in your organisation, you must first grapple with *whether* and *under what conditions* it should be deployed. That is the work of ethics.

## Why Ethics Comes Last — and Why That's Intentional

> *"Ethics is not an afterthought. It is the integrative lens through which all preceding modules must be refracted."*

Throughout this course, you've explored:
- How AI works (Module 1)
- What platforms and architectures exist (Modules 2 & 3)
- How to defend against AI-enabled threats (Module 4)
- Where AI adds value across business functions (Module 5)
- How to move from pilot to production (Module 6)
- Governance, compliance, and oversight (Module 7)

Now ethics asks the hardest questions of all:

> **Just because we *can* apply AI — should we? Under what terms? Who benefits? Who might be harmed?**

These are not abstract philosophical questions. They are practical leadership questions that every AI project eventually forces you to answer — whether you planned to or not.

# How This Module Connects to Your Capstone

Your capstone is not simply a technical document. It is a demonstration of **responsible leadership**.

A strong capstone proposal will:
- Identify a real business problem AI can address
- Choose the right technology for the context
- Plan for security, governance, and compliance
- Design for change management and human adoption
- Define meaningful KPIs to measure success
- And — crucially — **consider the ethical implications** for employees, customers, and society

The ethics section of this module gives you the lens. The capstone section shows you how to apply that lens in practice.
