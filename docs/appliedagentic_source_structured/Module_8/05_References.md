# Recommended Further Reading

- **"AI Fairness Isn't Just an Ethical Issue"** — Satell & Abdel-Magied (2020), Harvard Business Review — makes the business case for fairness in compelling terms
- **IAPP Resources on AI Governance** — iapp.org — the International Association of Privacy Professionals publishes extensive, practical guidance on privacy and AI governance
- **Partnership on AI** — partnershiponai.org — a multi-stakeholder organisation setting norms for responsible AI development and deployment
- **Google's Model Card Toolkit** — developers.google.com — practical tooling for creating model cards for AI systems
- **MIT Sloan Management Review: AI and the Future of Work** — sloanreview.mit.edu — ongoing research on workforce transformation and responsible AI leadership
- **"AI Will Transform the Global Economy"** — Georgieva (2024), IMF — essential reading for understanding the macro-economic and human stakes of AI adoption at scale
