## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A structured, free-to-read knowledge hub for learning and building agentic AI systems.

## Users
Repository evidence: engineers and AI professionals learning agentic systems and organizational transformation. Exact audience priorities remain open for the later implementation phase.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The existing product has learning paths, modules, lessons, search, audio, saved reading tools, and an administrative publishing workflow. Technical curricula cover Agentic AI Engineering, AI Evals, and Agent Harness Engineering; organizational transformation is also present.

The user requested three fully fresh mobile-first design previews, each with different layout, theme, fonts, visuals, navigation, and motion. No existing UI code, CSS, or structure may be reused. Preview only. No commits or publication. Buildless isolated previews are exploratory concepts, not a migration of production functionality. Progress, bookmarks, and sandbox runs in these previews are illustrative and local.

## Brand Commitments
Retain the Applied Agentic AI product name and subject matter. The user explicitly rejects a traditional blog and a persistent sidebar.
