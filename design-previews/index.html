APPLIED AGENTIC / A FRESH START

A new experience.
A deliberate transition.

Choose the direction by how it feels to explore, learn, and return. These three prototypes are built from scratch. Every design includes a working lesson preview.

Orbit

A connected knowledge atlas. Choose this for discovery, depth, and a sense of exploration.

Forma

A warm learning atelier. Choose this for focus, beautiful reading, and a human feel.

Current

A bright builder’s playground. Choose this for momentum, interaction, and learning by doing.

  1. Choose the experience

    Review discovery, a learning path, and the lesson reader at phone and desktop sizes. Select one direction, then settle the navigation and content hierarchy.

  2. Establish the new system

    Build new typography, color, spacing, motion, and accessible components in an isolated application shell. No old components or stylesheet imports.

  3. Rebuild the reader journey

    Connect existing content through a data boundary. Rebuild discovery, path overview, search, lessons, audio, and reading tools. Preserve published URLs and metadata.

  4. Rebuild the publishing workspace

    Create a fresh top-navigation admin workspace for content, media, analytics, and settings. Preserve publishing, AI tools, and permissions behind the new interface.

  5. Validate, then transition

    Check real long-form content, keyboard access, mobile reading, motion preferences, performance, and content parity in staging. Release after review, with a rollback path.

Preview scope

Interactions and learning progress are illustrative and stay in your browser. No backend, accounts, or production content have been changed. Admin redesign and data integration belong to the next phase.
